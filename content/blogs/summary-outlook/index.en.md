## Thoughts on Music
Any musical style that brings comfort, emotion, relaxation, joy, or allows listeners to see themselves in the song can be considered good music.  

I increasingly feel that songwriting shares much in common with writing. Both involve using technical skills and aesthetics to craft works that resonate with others.  

Contrast is crucial. Key changes create pitch contrast, variations in instrumentation bring dynamic contrast... Most pop music shares the same essence as classical symphonies—they're both "feasts of information". Chords and counterpoint form ever-changing interval relationships, creating irregularities within patterns, moving from tension to resolution, posing questions then answering them... The interplay of rhythmic grooves and melodic motion crafts pleasant tunes. When combined with emotionally charged or narrative lyrics, paired with colorful or deceptively simple chord progressions, this creates memorable auditory experiences.  

Ambient sounds play a significant role. Cicadas evoke endless summer days, vinyl crackles embody nostalgia, slightly detuned synth solos recall childhood birthday candles... Incorporating these elements builds unique atmospheres.

## About ICP Filing
Having maintained ICP filing for a year, my conclusion: {{<blackbox>}}Not worth the hassle{{</blackbox>}}  
While ICP filing enables Baidu indexing and domestic CDN access, it's server-dependent—problematic for static sites on Vercel. Baidu's ad-ridden search and strict policies make it less useful anyway. Vercel's built-in CDN suffices. For student bloggers, ICP filing proves time-consuming, costly, and ultimately ineffective.
